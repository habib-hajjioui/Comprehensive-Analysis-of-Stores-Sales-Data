# Comprehensive Analysis of Stores Sales Data

This is my first data analysis project, started in late 2023. I built it to learn how to work with real sales data, write SQL queries, and extract business insights from raw transactions. The dataset covers sales across multiple shopping malls and regions.
---

## Tools

- MySQL Workbench — data analysis and SQL queries
- Microsoft Excel — data exploration and reporting

---

## Dataset

The dataset contains sales transactions from shopping malls with the following fields:

- Customer Demographics: gender, age
- Transaction Details: quantity, selling price, cost price, payment method
- Geographical Data: region, state, shopping mall
- Product Info: category

---

## Key Questions and Findings

**Who spends more — men or women?**  
Female customers (39,186) outnumber males (26,349) by 48% and generate $97.9M in revenue compared to $67.3M for males. Women are the primary revenue driver across all categories.

**Which age groups shop the most?**  
The 20-40 age range has the highest customer count for both genders. The 18-20 group is the smallest. Customer numbers gradually decline after 50.

**Which product categories generate the most revenue?**  
Clothing leads at $75M, followed by Shoes at $43.6M and Technology at $38M. Food, Books, and Souvenirs generate significantly less revenue.

**What is the average age of shoppers per category?**  
All categories attract customers in a very narrow age range — between 43.2 and 43.6 years. Age alone is not a useful differentiator between product categories.

**Who are the top spending customers?**  
All top 5 customers purchased Technology products. The highest single transaction was $47,250. High-value buyers tend to shop at premium mall locations.

**Which regions perform best?**  
The West region leads with $52.8M, followed by the East at $47.3M. The South generates the least at $26.6M — nearly half of the West.

**Which states generate the most revenue?**  
California leads at $32M, more than New York and Texas combined. Revenue is heavily concentrated in a small number of states.

**What payment methods do customers prefer?**  
Cash is the most used method with 29,191 transactions. However credit card users spend significantly more per transaction on average.
